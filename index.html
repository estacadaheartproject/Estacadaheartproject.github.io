Estacada Heart Project

Supporting the Estacada community with heart.

Estacada Heart Project provides support to the Estacada School District Community region through projects in a meaningful, heartfelt, and impactful manner.

About Estacada Heart Project

Estacada Heart Project is a community-focused charitable nonprofit serving the Estacada School District Community region. Our work brings volunteers, donors, sponsors, and community members together to support local individuals and families through meaningful projects.

This corporation is organized exclusively for charitable purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.
